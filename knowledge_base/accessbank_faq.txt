# AccessBank Knowledge Base

## General Information
- AccessBank is one of Azerbaijan's leading microfinance banks, headquartered in Baku.
- AccessBank serves individuals, entrepreneurs, and small-to-medium businesses.
- Official website: https://www.accessbank.az
- Customer support hotline: *8801 (free from mobile) or +994 12 310 88 01
- Email: info@accessbank.az

## Working Hours
- Branches: Monday to Friday, 09:00 – 18:00
- Some branches open on Saturday: 09:00 – 14:00
- Branches are closed on Sundays and public holidays
- Online banking and ATMs are available 24/7

## Branch Network
- AccessBank has branches across Azerbaijan including Baku, Ganja, Sumgait, Lankaran, Sheki, and other cities.
- To find the nearest branch: visit https://www.accessbank.az/en/branches

## Mobile Banking & Internet Banking
- AccessBank mobile app is available on iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play)
- Internet banking portal: https://ibank.accessbank.az
- To register: visit a branch with your ID or register through the mobile app
- For login issues: call *8801 or visit the nearest branch
- OTP codes are sent to the phone number registered with your account

## Cards
- AccessBank issues Visa and Mastercard debit and credit cards
- Cards can be ordered at any branch or through the mobile app
- Standard card delivery: 3-5 business days
- For lost or stolen cards: immediately call *8801 to block the card
- Card blocking is available 24/7 via the hotline
- ATM withdrawal limits: up to 1000 AZN per day (standard)

## Transfers & Payments
- Transfers between AccessBank accounts: instant and free
- Transfers to other banks in Azerbaijan: processed within 1 business day
- International transfers: SWIFT transfers available, 2-3 business days
- Payment services: utility bills, mobile top-up, taxes available via mobile app
- Transfer limits: up to 10,000 AZN per day via mobile banking

## Loans & Credit Products
- AccessBank offers personal loans, business loans, mortgage loans, and consumer loans
- Loan application can be submitted online or at any branch
- Required documents: ID card, tax ID (VÖEN), proof of income
- Loan decisions: typically within 1-3 business days
- Minimum loan amount: 200 AZN
- Maximum personal loan: up to 50,000 AZN depending on income
- Loan repayment: via mobile app, internet banking, or branch

## Deposits & Savings
- AccessBank offers term deposits in AZN, USD, and EUR
- Interest rates vary by term and currency
- Minimum deposit: 100 AZN / 100 USD / 100 EUR
- Deposits are insured under the Azerbaijan Deposit Insurance Fund (ADIF)

## Currency Exchange
- Currency exchange available at all branches
- Rates are updated daily and available on the website
- Online currency exchange available via mobile banking

## Security & Safety
- AccessBank will NEVER ask for your PIN, CVV, OTP, password, or full card number
- If someone asks for this information claiming to be AccessBank, it is a scam
- Report suspicious activity immediately to *8801

## Account Opening
- Individual accounts can be opened at any branch with a valid ID
- Business accounts require additional documentation
- Students and young people (18+) can open accounts with just an ID card

## Fees & Commissions
- Account maintenance: free for standard accounts
- Card issuance: free for first card
- ATM withdrawals at AccessBank ATMs: free
- ATM withdrawals at other bank ATMs: 1% commission (min 0.50 AZN)
- International transfers: fees apply depending on amount and destination
